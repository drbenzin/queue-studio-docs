Refund Policy

Effective 24 September 2026.

You can ask for a refund of any payment for Queue Studio for Service Bus Pro within 14 days after it was made, for any reason, whether it was the first purchase or a renewal. The payment is refunded in full, including tax, through Polar, the merchant of record, to the original payment method.

How to ask

  • Email factodus@gmail.com from the address you used at checkout, or reply to your Polar receipt email.
  • Include the order number or the email address of the purchase.

What happens

  • The refund is issued through Polar, and the subscription is cancelled at the same time, so its license key stops working. The free features keep working.
  • How long the money takes to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.

After 14 days

Payments for the current or past periods are not refunded, except where the law requires it. Cancelling in the customer portal stops future renewals; the key keeps working until the end of the paid period.

Polar and chargebacks

Polar may also refund a payment on its own under its buyer terms. Please write to us before opening a chargeback with your bank; we will resolve the problem or refund within the window above.

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have as a consumer under the law of your country that cannot be limited by contract. See also the terms of sale.
